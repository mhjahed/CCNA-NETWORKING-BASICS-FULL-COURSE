How to use this site

1 · Read

The study guide condenses all 17 modules of your course into key ideas and terms, each linked to the simulator that demonstrates it.

2 · Do

The simulators recreate the Packet Tracer activities in your browser — configure the router, lease DHCP addresses, watch NAT and ARP behave.

3 · Verify

Lab guides carry checklists and model answers; the quiz reports against the same module bands as your knowledge check.
